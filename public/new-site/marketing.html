Questions

Which ad platforms does it connect to?

Facebook, Instagram, Google and the platforms you already use. We connect them when we set up your system.

Is the WhatsApp message from our number?

Yes. Replies go out from your business number, in your words.

Is Lead Memory a newsletter?

No. Nothing is emailed. Your site changes for that visitor when they come back.

What if nobody calls within 5 minutes?

The lead moves to the next rep and the manager is told. You choose the time limit.

Is our data private?

Yes. Your leads live in your own system, on your own domain and server.

What does it cost?

You pay for the marketing back end and the new website, then a monthly subscription. Book a demo for a clear number.
