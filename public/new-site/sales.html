Questions

Do my reps need new phones or apps?

No. They call from the CRM in the browser. A headset helps, nothing else is needed.

Can Igani hear our calls?

Your calls are private. They're recorded and reviewed inside your own system, on your own domain and server. For the free demo, we only see the one recording you choose to send us.

Is recording calls allowed?

Recording rules differ by country. We set recording up with you for where you call, and recommend checking with your own legal advisor.

What languages does it support?

Calls and reviews are in English today.

How accurate is the review?

It's an AI reading of the transcript, written to help coaching. Every point comes with the quote and the time, so anyone can check it.

What does it cost?

You pay for the CRM and the new website and back end, then a monthly subscription, based on your team size and features. Book a demo for a clear number.
