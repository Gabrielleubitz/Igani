Questions

What does it cost?

You pay for the website and the back end, then a monthly subscription. The price depends on your team size and the features you choose. Book a consultation and we'll give you a clear number.

Can Igani hear our calls?

Your calls are private. They're recorded and reviewed inside your own system, on your own domain and server. For the free demo, we only see the one recording you choose to send us.

Is our data separate from other companies?

Yes. Each company gets its own separate system, on its own domain and its own server.

Can I start small?

Yes. Most clients start with the part that costs them most, and add more later.

What languages do you support?

Your site can be in any language. Call review works with calls in any language too.
