Questions

Does the AI write the contract itself?

No. It fills in your own lawyer-approved templates with what was agreed. You check it before it's sent.

Are e-signatures legally valid?

We set up signing with you and your lawyer, for the documents and the country you work in.

Which meeting tools does it work with?

Google Meet today.

Where do Ask Your Site's answers come from?

Only from your own site, CRM and campaigns. It says when it doesn't know.

Is our data private?

Yes. Your calls, meetings and contracts live in your own system, on your own domain and server.
