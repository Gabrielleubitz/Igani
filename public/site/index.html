Questions

We already use a CRM. Do we start over?

No. Igani connects to the tools you already use. Start with one part, add the rest later.

My team won't learn another tool.

They won't have to learn eight. One login: leads, calls, contracts.

What if it doesn't work for us?

You see a free demo on your own business first, and pay ₪0 before the contract.

How fast is it live?

1–2 weeks for a base site, 2–4 depending on complexity.

What does it cost?

Your website, then a monthly fee based on team size and features. You get a clear number in the first meeting.

Can you hear our calls?

No. Your calls and data stay in your own system, on your own server. We only hear a call if you choose to send it to us.
